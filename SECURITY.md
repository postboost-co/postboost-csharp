# Security policy

Please do **not** report security vulnerabilities in public issues.

Email **security@postboost.co** with a description, steps to reproduce and the affected version. We acknowledge reports within 3 business days and keep you updated until the issue is fixed.

We ask for up to 90 days before public disclosure, and we credit reporters who want to be named.

Out of scope: findings that need a stolen API token, social engineering, and denial of service.
