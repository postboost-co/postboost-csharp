# Code of Conduct

PostBoost projects follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hi@postboost.co**. Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove comments, close issues or block participants who break this code.
